Lineage: who inspired whom

Frameworks don't descend from one another in a tree. Ideas do. Follow any single idea through time and you get a clean line that wanders in and out of a dozen projects.

Seven threads are traced below. Each chip links to the framework's entry. A yellow chip marks the point where a thread returns to an idea it had earlier abandoned; those are examined on the loops page.

Three patterns in the table

The influential projects usually aren't the popular ones. Knockout, Elm, Ember, Meteor, Solid and Qwik each had modest adoption at best. Each supplied an idea that a larger project later took mainstream. The winners were mostly synthesisers: jQuery, React, Vue and Next.js all assembled existing ideas with better ergonomics.

The same people keep reappearing. Miško Hevery made AngularJS and then Qwik, which repudiates it. Rich Harris made Ractive, Rollup and Svelte. Evan You made Vue and Vite. Ryan Florence went from Ember to React Router to Remix. Steve Sanderson went from Knockout to Blazor. Much of this history is a few dozen people changing their minds in public.

What the browser absorbs, dies. jQuery's selectors, Prototype's array methods, Dojo's promises and modules, CoffeeScript's arrow functions and classes all moved into the platform, and the libraries lost their reason to exist. It's the most honourable way for a library to end.
